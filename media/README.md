# The Forest of Qubits — illustrated learning series

In addition to the three original YouTube stories linked by this project, a longer illustrated explanation was produced in Portuguese, English and Spanish. Each version contains the same 16 chapters and runs approximately 9 minutes 30 seconds, with chapter transitions aligned across languages. Each chapter is also supplied as an independent video.

The new series covers bits, qubits, amplitudes, phase, measurement, gates, interference, entanglement, communication limits, hardware, noise, a limited repetition-code example, potential applications, and the video-linked Qiskit learning lab.

The full scripts are available in [Portuguese](scripts/pt.md), [English](scripts/en.md) and [Spanish](scripts/es.md). These scripts were prepared with Codex assistance during the hackathon window. The illustration was generated with image_gen, and synthetic narration was used. The reference was a participant-supplied 30-second animated forest clip. The new production uses an original illustration, camera movement, explanatory diagrams and captions.

The 48 individual chapters are publicly available in [media/chapters](chapters/README.md), organized by language, with direct MP4 links and editable captions. The three full-length versions were delivered locally and are not included in this upload. These educational chapters do not replace the separate 2–3 minute demo of the runnable project required by the hackathon. The three original public YouTube links remain in the main README.
